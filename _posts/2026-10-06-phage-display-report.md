The [Nanobody Discovery Summer School](https://biochorl.github.io/Nanobody_Discovery_Summer_School/) was aimed at training international young researchers in nanobody-based technologies to develop reagents for targeting antigens, by offering experimental experience in library construction and phage display panning and lectures on current computational and AI-based methods for structure prediction, screening, molecular dynamics and *de novo* design. 
A second aim was to build connections for future collaborations.
Each of 9 participants received a Certificate of Attendance stating the workload being 30 h (1 ECTS).

Summer school was supported by Instruct.SI.
