**Would you like to bring computational protein design into your experimental research?**

The **University of Ljubljana, Biotechnical Faculty**, and **Instruct.SI** invite you to a hands-on workshop connecting experimental biochemistry and structural biology with modern computational methods for protein design.

- *When?* **17–18 December 2026**  
- *Where?* **Department of Biology, Biotechnical Faculty, University of Ljubljana** ([Večna pot 111, Ljubljana, Slovenia](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=46.051158&mlon=14.469895#map=17/46.051158/14.469890&layers=P))
- *Until when I need to apply?* **Application deadline: 15 November 2026** via [**this online form**](https://tinyurl.com/2rhmh6br)

Through practical exercises, you will explore modern software and deep learning tools and learn how to apply them in your own research projects.

---

**What will you learn?**

- **Backbone design:** use diffusion models to generate new protein folds or adapt natural ones.
- **Sequence design:** use inverse-folding models to design sequences for new backbones or improve existing proteins.
- **Screening:** use structure prediction and protein language models to identify promising candidates.
- **Case study:** bring these approaches together.

The workshop will be led by **Dr. Béla Frohn**, postdoctoral researcher at the AITHYRA Institute, with guest lecturer **Dr. Ajasja Ljubetič**, Research Assistant Professor at the National Institute of Chemistry.

---

**Who should apply?**

Researchers at all career stages, especially young scientists, with basic knowledge of structural biology who wish to acquire or strengthen practical skills in computational protein design.

**Participation is limited to 20 attendees**, selected based on their CV and motivation letter.

**Submit your CV and motivation letter by 15 November 2026:** [Apply here!](https://tinyurl.com/2rhmh6br)
