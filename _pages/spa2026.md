We are pleased to announce the successful completion of the Instruct.SI CryoEM Workshop 2026, Single Particle Analysis, organised by the Instruct.SI consortium from 10–12 November 2026 at the National Institute of Chemistry, Ljubljana. Eight highly enthusiastic participants from Austria, the Czech Republic, Italy, Portugal, Slovenia and the United Kingdom brought their SPA samples, vitrified them and screened them on a cryo-TEM. The participants now have a good understanding of how SPA is carried out from start to finish, and we believe that they have the basic knowledge and tools to confidently start or continue work on SPA projects.

The workshop would not have been possible without the experienced mentors who guided the participants through the theoretical background, hands-on sessions and data analysis on a HPC.

Positive and constructive feedback will help us organise future workshops and events tailored to researchers at different stages in their careers who want to learn more about the exciting world of CryoEM.

**Researchers who wish to deepen their understanding of cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) are cordially invited to attend the Instruct.SI Cryo-EM Workshop on Single Particle Analysis.**

![Invitation SPA2026]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/spa2026/SPA2026_EN_v1.png)

Cryo-EM Workshop on Single Particle Analysis (SPA) is based on the very positive response to the [Basics of Methodological Approaches in Structural Biology workshop in 2024](https://instruct-eric.si/bmasb2024) and the and [Instruct.SI CryoEM Advanced Workshop 2025](https://instruct-eric.si/spa2025). This year, Instruct.SI and the National Institute of Chemistry are pleased to invite applications for an in-person cryo-EM workshop designed to provide researchers with meaningful, hands-on experience covering nearly the entire cryo-EM workflow - from EM grid preparation to 3D reconstruction. 


**The workshop program will include:**
 <ul>
  <li>Theoretical background of the cryo-EM method</li>
  <li>Practical work in small, mentored teams</li>
  <li>Experimental sessions will include grid preparation with TFS Vitrobot Mk IV and data acquisition with TFS Glacios (sample loading, data collection)</li>
  <li>Cryo-EM data processing and analysis with 3D reconstruction</li>
  <li>Networking opportunities with experts from the field and other cryo-EM users</li>
</ul> 
<br>

**Updated programme** <br>
![Programme v3]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/spa2026/Programme_SPA2026_v3.png)

**Date and place** <br>
The workshop will be held **from 10<sup>th</sup> to 12<sup>th</sup> November 2026** at the **National Institute of Chemistry**, [Hajdrihova ulica 19, Ljubljana, Slovenia](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=46.042711&mlon=14.493613#map=19/46.042711/14.493613).
<br>

**Who should apply:**<br>
This workshop is intended for **researchers at all career stages**, especially young scientists (BSc and PhD students, postdocs), who have basic knowledge of structural biology and wish to acquire or improve practical skills in cryo-EM. <br>
Up to eight (8) attendees will be selected based on their **CV and motivation letter** to ensure a high-quality hands-on experience. **We especially encourage young researchers from Slovenia and the neighboring region to apply.**  Applicants will have the opportunity to briefly present their research and bring their own samples. Up to two of these samples will be selected for cryo-EM analysis during the workshop, based on their quality and suitability.
